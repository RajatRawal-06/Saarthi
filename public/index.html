FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Saarthi

Simple, honest answers to common questions about quick-commerce optimization.

How does Saarthi calculate the cheapest total basket across multiple stores? ⌄
Saarthi uses an integer linear optimization engine. Rather than simply picking the store with the cheapest raw milk price, it factors in handling charges, delivery fee thresholds (e.g. ₹199 on Blinkit vs ₹149 on Zepto), and small-cart penalties. If splitting between two stores saves money even after both handling charges, Saarthi prepares a split-cart recommendation with one-click links.
Can I type my grocery list in natural everyday Hinglish? ⌄
Yes! Our natural language parser is specifically calibrated for everyday Indian grocery terminology. Phrases like "doodh 2 packet, 1kg tamatar, bread, maggi 4 pack, aashirvaad atta 5kg" are automatically parsed into precise commodities, standard weight units, and brand requirements.
Does Saarthi have access to my bank account or credit card? ⌄
Never. Saarthi does not touch, request, or store your payment cards, UPI PINs, or net banking credentials. The agent's role stops after preparing your items into your basket. You complete checkout yourself on the official Blinkit, Zepto, or Swiggy app with your trusted UPI payment provider.
What if an item is out of stock at my local dark store? ⌄
Saarthi checks live inventory at dark stores physically servicing your exact PIN code. If an item is out of stock on one app, the engine dynamically re-routes that item to another store where it is readily available, so your kitchen list is never left unfulfilled.

ORDERING FAQ

Common questions about automated orders

Clear details on how Saarthi prepares orders and protects your payment information.

How does Saarthi find the right product without my store password? ⌄
Saarthi uses headless browser agents powered by TinyFish to search the public catalog of stores servicing your PIN code. When you approve the proposed match, Saarthi opens the official product listing with your prepared cart so you can log in securely via your own OTP.
What happens if an exact pack size is out of stock? ⌄
Saarthi looks for the closest available unit match (e.g. two 250ml bottles instead of one 500ml bottle) and explicitly highlights this in the match confirmation card with an attention badge before anything is finalized.
Can I order from multiple stores in a single natural language sentence? ⌄
Yes! If you mention multiple stores (e.g. "2 amul butter from blinkit and dolo 650 from tata 1mg"), Saarthi parses each item with its target store into an organized multi-store plan.
Does Saarthi save my payment cards or UPI credentials? ⌄
No. Saarthi is strictly non-custodial. We never request, store, or process credit cards, debit cards, or UPI PINs. You make all payments directly on the official merchant gateway.
