PRICE DISCOVERY FAQ

Common questions about live price comparisons

Everything you need to know about stealth scraping, price guarantees, and algorithmic pricing.

Why do prices differ so significantly between apps for the exact same item? ⌄
Retail apps employ algorithmic pricing driven by proximity to local warehouses, real-time local demand, seller commission rates (e.g. Amazon seller fees vs wholesale direct), and localized subsidies. A consumer buying without checking frequently pays 15% to 25% higher simply due to app inertia.
How does Saarthi catch hidden "drip pricing" and checkout surcharges? ⌄
Many platforms advertise a low base price on product search pages, then append "packaging charges", "handling fees", and "rain/surge delivery fees" on the checkout page. Saarthi inspects the actual order summary elements in the browser session so you always see the true out-of-pocket total.
Can I use Saarthi's verified proof screenshots for price-match claims? ⌄
Yes! Every row includes a "View proof" button that opens an immutable screenshot artifact with the exact store name, product title, seller badge, timestamp, and landed price. Many offline retailers (like Croma and Reliance Digital) will match competitor pricing when presented with verified proof.
Does searching for a product multiple times trigger surge pricing algorithms? ⌄
No. Because Saarthi uses ephemeral headless browser containers with distinct user sessions and rotating IP pools, retailers cannot link searches to your personal identity, device fingerprint, or browsing history. You are completely protected from algorithmic price gouging.
