FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Saarthi

Simple, honest answers to common questions about quick-commerce optimization.

How does Saarthi calculate the cheapest total basket across multiple stores? ⌄
Saarthi uses an integer linear optimization engine. Rather than simply picking the store with the cheapest raw milk price, it factors in handling charges, delivery fee thresholds (e.g. ₹199 on Blinkit vs ₹149 on Zepto), and small-cart penalties. If splitting between two stores saves money even after both handling charges, Saarthi prepares a split-cart recommendation with one-click links.
Can I type my grocery list in natural everyday Hinglish? ⌄
Yes! Our natural language parser is specifically calibrated for everyday Indian grocery terminology. Phrases like "doodh 2 packet, 1kg tamatar, bread, maggi 4 pack, aashirvaad atta 5kg" are automatically parsed into precise commodities, standard weight units, and brand requirements.
Does Saarthi have access to my bank account or credit card? ⌄
Never. Saarthi does not touch, request, or store your payment cards, UPI PINs, or net banking credentials. The agent's role stops after preparing your items into your basket. You complete checkout yourself on the official Blinkit, Zepto, or Swiggy app with your trusted UPI payment provider.
What if an item is out of stock at my local dark store? ⌄
Saarthi checks live inventory at dark stores physically servicing your exact PIN code. If an item is out of stock on one app, the engine dynamically re-routes that item to another store where it is readily available, so your kitchen list is never left unfulfilled.

PRICE DISCOVERY FAQ

Common questions about live price comparisons

Everything you need to know about stealth scraping, price guarantees, and algorithmic pricing.

Why do prices differ so significantly between apps for the exact same item? ⌄
Retail apps employ algorithmic pricing driven by proximity to local warehouses, real-time local demand, seller commission rates (e.g. Amazon seller fees vs wholesale direct), and localized subsidies. A consumer buying without checking frequently pays 15% to 25% higher simply due to app inertia.
How does Saarthi catch hidden "drip pricing" and checkout surcharges? ⌄
Many platforms advertise a low base price on product search pages, then append "packaging charges", "handling fees", and "rain/surge delivery fees" on the checkout page. Saarthi inspects the actual order summary elements in the browser session so you always see the true out-of-pocket total.
Can I use Saarthi's verified proof screenshots for price-match claims? ⌄
Yes! Every row includes a "View proof" button that opens an immutable screenshot artifact with the exact store name, product title, seller badge, timestamp, and landed price. Many offline retailers (like Croma and Reliance Digital) will match competitor pricing when presented with verified proof.
Does searching for a product multiple times trigger surge pricing algorithms? ⌄
No. Because Saarthi uses ephemeral headless browser containers with distinct user sessions and rotating IP pools, retailers cannot link searches to your personal identity, device fingerprint, or browsing history. You are completely protected from algorithmic price gouging.

How long are audit screenshots and session recordings preserved? ⌄
Session screenshots and telemetry logs are saved locally in your active workspace directory. They remain accessible indefinitely until you explicitly delete an item using the delete (✕) button or purge the jobs file.
How does Saarthi calculate TinyFish agent compute costs? ⌄
TinyFish charges per headless browser session duration (typically $0.06 to $0.20 per store sweep). Saarthi calculates exact execution milliseconds and logs the accurate dollar amount directly from the API response into your activity ledger.
What happens when I click the "Re-run" button on an old job? ⌄
Clicking "Re-run" immediately creates a fresh autonomous task with the exact same parameters (product name, store targets, PIN code) and spins up live browser agents to check current prices right now, displaying the live execution stream.
What does an "Interrupted" or "Partial Results" status mean? ⌄
An "Interrupted" status occurs if a retailer dark store was temporarily offline (e.g. late night closed hours), or if an aggressive CAPTCHA required human verification. "Partial Results" indicates that several stores succeeded while one timed out.
